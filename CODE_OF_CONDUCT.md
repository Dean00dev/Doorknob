# Community code of conduct

Doorknob welcomes respectful contributions from patients, disabled people,
clinicians, carers, designers, engineers, and critics.

Be constructive, specific, and considerate. Treat lived experience as expertise.
Do not publish another person's medical information. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, sexual attention, deliberate humiliation, or disclosure of private
information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove participation that violates these expectations
and may restrict repeated or serious abuse. Sensitive conduct reports should use
the repository's private reporting route and begin the title with `COMMUNITY`.
